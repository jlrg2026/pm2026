About

Engineering Leadership That Delivers

From strategy to start-up, I lead major capital investment projects end-to-end with full accountability for cost, schedule, quality, safety, permitting, and operational readiness.

I am a Senior Project Manager with over a decade of experience delivering major capital investment projects in complex, multinational environments. My career spans FMCG (PepsiCo), Financial Services (MUFG, BBVA, Banorte), Healthcare IT (Cegedim), and Global Consulting (Stefanini, BlueYonder).

My approach combines rigorous project engineering governance — scope definition, estimating, scheduling, stage-gate documentation, and change control — with high-performance team leadership. I build and lead multidisciplinary teams of internal specialists, external contractors, and vendors under Agile, SAFe, Waterfall, and PMO frameworks.

I am known for turning complexity into execution: aligning cross-functional partners in Quality, SHE, Sourcing, and Manufacturing; engaging external stakeholders such as permitting authorities, utilities, and governmental bodies; and delivering transparent, data-driven reporting to senior leadership.
